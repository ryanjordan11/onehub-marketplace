# OneHub Build Launch Thumbnail

Create a square, high-energy launch thumbnail for OneHub.

Ask for a subject photo, a short kicker, a large headline, supporting copy, and an optional callout. Keep the supplied person's identity recognizable. Place the person on the right and reserve the left side for large, readable copy.

Use a dramatic black background with electric blue interface light, white and yellow headline text, and a small OneHub logo treatment. The result should feel bold, cinematic, and immediately legible at thumbnail size.
